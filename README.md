# COMP1004-CW

Remaking Peggle in HTML.

Peggle is a pachinko style game in which you fire balls at orange pegs to clear a stage. Each stage consists of a layout of pegs. Peg colours consist of blue, orange, purple and green pegs. When a peg is hit it is removed after the ball disappears. You have 10 shots to clear a stage. If a player scores 25,000 points on a shot, they earn a free ball. They can get another ball at 75,000 points in the same shot, and a third at 125,000 points, if the bucket is hit or if no pegs are hit and you win a 50/50 chance you’ll gain a shot. To clear the stage, you need to destroy the 25 orange pegs. A score is gained by hitting pegs. Blue and green pegs are worth 10, orange is worth 100 and purple are worth 500 (this is without the score multiplier). When the orange pegs are cleared the bottom of the screen is segmented into two 10,000 holes, two 50,000 holes and a sole 100,000 hole in the center of the screen (this is called extreme fever), and wherever the final ball lands you gain that number of points. Green pegs activate the effect of the character being used. Fever meters are tracked by the amount of orange pegs hit in one shot. Fever meter goes to x2, x3, x5, and x10.

In this project I will remake the first level of Peggle. Not all of the assets from peggle will be used.

Things that won't be implemented:
Character abilities.
Green pegs.
Grid at bottom of screen after final orange peg is hit.
Style points.
Bucket trick shots.
Bucket collisions (ball can’t bounce off the side of the bucket).
